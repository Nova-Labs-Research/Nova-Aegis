# Conceptual Heritage

Nova Aegis draws inspiration from several families of ideas: evidence and provenance systems, graph representations, associative memory, attention and salience, contradiction-aware reasoning, local-first software, and capability-oriented governance.

The previous Nova Labs projects referenced in the architecture documents are conceptual references, not required dependencies or direct implementation sources. This document records conceptual influence, not a claim of direct implementation, scientific validation, or equivalence to any cited tradition.

Future additions should state which mechanism is being borrowed and which assumptions do not transfer. Domain-specific material, including UAP, Roswell, investigation-specific, and dataset-specific content, remains outside Nova Aegis.
