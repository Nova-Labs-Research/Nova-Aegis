# Problem statement

AI-assisted systems often make uncertain material look settled. They can lose the origin of an observation, merge interpretations with source material, treat repeated references as corroboration, and optimize for a fluent answer rather than a reviewable chain of reasoning.

Nova Aegis investigates a narrower question:

> How might a local-first cognitive architecture coordinate evidence, memory, association, bounded reasoning, governance, and discourse while keeping uncertainty and provenance visible?

The research problem has two sides:

1. **Cognitive organization:** represent and retrieve noisy, incomplete, contradictory material without requiring premature resolution.
2. **Governed execution:** keep identity, authorization, routing, persistence, and audit boundaries explicit when a system handles that material.

The project is not trying to establish truth automatically, replace human review, or turn uncertain evidence into operational conclusions. A useful result is one that makes uncertainty, lineage, disagreement, and limits easier to inspect.
