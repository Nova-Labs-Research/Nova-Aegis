# Nova Aegis Phase 69 - Review Burden and Fairness

## Experiment

Evaluate synthetic routing workloads by baseline accuracy, reliability accuracy, false route changes, genuine improvements, fallback count, and review burden. Metrics are descriptive and cannot justify trusted reliability routing without representative workloads and protected provenance.

## Decision

`DEFER` reliability adoption. Preserve baseline-fail-closed routing and require human review for consequential decisions.
